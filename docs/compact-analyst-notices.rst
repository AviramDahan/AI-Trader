Compact analyst notices
=======================

Broad analyst updates use the existing analyst-topic flag and destination.
The canonical and legacy broad-news presenters show the translated headline,
verified tickers when available, and complementary source-backed rating/target
text. Repeated headline text, template boilerplate and trader interpretation are
omitted from this public presentation. Original evidence, analysis, publication
checks and durable delivery keys are preserved. Personal alerts and financial
results keep their existing templates. Publication times and required license
credits remain; no new inference request or upside calculation is introduced.

Synthetic example (not a delivered notice)::

    📊 UBS העלה את מחיר היעד של חברת דוגמה מ־220 ל־250 דולר

    מניות: SYN

    ההמלצה נשארה קנייה.

    פורסם: 06/11/2026 16:00

Compaction retains complementary summary text rather than guessing its facts
from keywords or cutting arbitrary characters. Multiple broker actions can exceed the usual
short notice length. The existing final Telegram UTF-16/RTL presentation limit
still applies. This change does not edit, delete or resend past messages.
