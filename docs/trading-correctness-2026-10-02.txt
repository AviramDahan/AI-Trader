MANUAL RESOLUTION PROCEDURE (DESIGN ONLY, NOT PERFORMED)
Require an authorized operator and explicit per-order approval. Preserve order,
signal, cursor, account and fill evidence plus the gap interval, source candle
timestamps, completion/session/timezone evidence and provider retrieval timestamps.
Obtain ALL completed regular-session bars covering creation through the earliest
possible fill/expiry; partial boundary candles alone cannot establish sequencing.
Record decision, reviewer, reason, evidence hash and before/after ledger reconciliation
in a protected incident record. If chronology remains ambiguous, retain hold.
Never infer no-fill just from elapsed time. Never reset terminal orders or rewind a
global cursor (that could replay unrelated positions). A proven fill requires a
separately reviewed isolated replay and idempotent transactional application; no
automatic remediation tool is included. A proven no-fill may be explicitly resolved
only with full evidence and exposure/cash checks. Do not edit production in this task.
