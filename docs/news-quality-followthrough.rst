News quality followthrough
=========================

Scope and evidence
------------------

This is an isolated implementation following the23October2026 manual audit,
not a new source subscription, trading-policy change or publication replay.
The audit's115 ``identity_unverified`` observations are not115 worthwhile
articles. A single bounded retained read separates publisher membership and
12 deterministic examples; the current company catalog is not invented as a
historical ingestion catalog. Private observations are not included in Git.

Hebrew translation
------------------

``trump_truth_translation.local_json`` sends native JSON characters rather than
escaping every Hebrew character into six ASCII characters. SOURCE and DRAFT
are logically unchanged. An isolated full-translation/editor test exercises the
real existing local-capacity guard with mocked transport: the editor payload
falls from9476 to4356 UTF-8 bytes for that fixture. A truly oversized native
request still fails before generation. The model, provider,1400 output limit,
8192 context policy, factual/editor checks, leases, two-attempt queue policy,
cache binding and zero paid fallback remain unchanged.

This proves avoidable input inflation, not the cause of32 historical unavailable
notices, live model fluency or a guaranteed translation success rate. Existing
unavailable/sent messages are not requeued or republished.

Company identity
----------------

Explicit ticker matching must consume a complete identifier: ``$AAPLjunk`` is
not ``$AAPL``. The suffix now rejects lowercase/Unicode letters and underscores
as well as uppercase letters/digits. Exact exchange/cashtag, CIK, legal-name and
Yahoo primary-subject requirements retain their existing authority. Related
tickers alone, bare short names, outsiders and roundups do not become verified
company identities. This safety fix does not claim to solve all115 blocks.

Reported macro bulletins
------------------------

The retained sample exposed two complete numeric-release forms incorrectly
classified as unknown company news: French manufacturing PMI flash and UK
retail-sales YoY/MoM. ``macro_rules.reported_economic_release`` recognizes only
the anchored country/series plus literal ACTUAL, FORECAST and PREVIOUS numeric
tuple (percent units mandatory for retail). Corporate names, previews,
incomplete tuples and added opinion do not match. It supplies neither a missing
reported month/year nor a new release/deduplication identity. This is macro
classification, not a relaxed company identity gate or source-truth claim.

New unseen matching bulletins can reach the existing factual and editorial
checks; low materiality/relevance, stale or unapproved content still cannot
publish. Old blocked records retain their existing unchanged-source dedupe
state and are not automatically replayed. Two examples out of12 are not an
estimate of how many of all115 blocks should publish. No quantity quota,
publisher-universe expansion or new AI calls per review is introduced.

Public source presentation
--------------------------

``news_events.runtime.message`` selects one retained approved source URL,
preferring an official/regulatory source when available. It does not invent an
original article behind a relay or use AI text as provenance. A linked source
is not a claim that its content was fetched or its assertions independently
verified. With only a relay or an unsafe/non-article URL, no article link is
fabricated. Trump uses its separate existing original-post renderer.

``news_source_links`` accepts bounded public HTTPS DNS article/filing links,
rejecting credentials, IP/local hosts, invalid ports, control characters,
relay/social links and secret query fields. Tracking query fields/fragments are
removed without removing article identity fields. No link is fetched. AI draft
URLs/source-label lines and injected publisher/license-label links cannot
nominate the canonical source footer. Required licensed credit is preserved.

``telegram_topics.with_news_community_link`` preserves the explicit safe source
footer and existing community/license links while hiding inline/relay links.
The final bidi-rendered UTF-16 budget reserves source and community footers,
not merely a pre-bidi character count. Long text is explicitly clipped without
cutting a URL, number or isolate. Topic routing and non-news/trade messages do
not change. Evidence, dedupe keys, publication eligibility and AI inputs remain
unchanged by this presentation path.

Release boundary
----------------

No schema, snapshot, flags, source activation boundary or recovery contract is
changed. The deployment process, safety checks, risk/News/AI/trading controls,
automations and Telegram permissions remain unchanged. Feature-branch/PR CI
does not deploy; merge/deployment still require the human's reviewed release
approval. Existing PostgreSQL Readiness supplies the real isolated DB gate.
Local skipped PostgreSQL tests are not reported as a pass. Natural publication
and quantity/quality improvement require post-release observation, not a
synthetic Production message or historical resend.
